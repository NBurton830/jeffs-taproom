# 20 — Jeff's Big Burger Challenge `/burger-challenge`

A signature attraction that deserves its own page. It's shareable, gets local press, and it's an SEO magnet.

- **BBC-001 (P1)** The hero shows "Jeff's Big Burger Challenge", the price **$35.99** and "Finish it in 30 minutes and it's
  free."
- **BBC-002 (P1)** **What's in it** list (region named "What's in it"): 3 cheeseburgers, 2 Bacon Grilled Cheese Sandwiches, 1/2 lb fried onion petals,
  pickles, lettuce, tomato, ketchup, and 1 lb krinkle-cut fries.
- **BBC-003 (P1)** **Rules** as an ordered list (region named "Rules"): eat all the food, keep it down, 30 minutes, dine-in only. Each rule is
  CMS-editable.
- **BBC-004 (P1)** **Share the Challenge Burger** callout: $49.99, in-house only.
- **BBC-005 (P1)** **Hall of Fame** (`data-testid="hall-of-fame"`): approved winners sorted by fastest time
  (`mm:ss`), then the latest date. **Wall of Valiant Attempts**
  (`data-testid="valiant-attempts"`) for `attempted`. Each entry is a `<li>` showing the name and, for winners, the time. Entries without approval never show.
- **BBC-006 (P1)** CTA "Call to schedule your attempt" (tel link). There is no online ordering for this item (consistent
  with MENU-006).
- **BBC-007 (P2)** Built-in 30-minute countdown timer for staff, displayed on the bar TV.
